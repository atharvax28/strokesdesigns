Born from Experience.
Built for Scale.

Not to change the system, but to be an integral part of it.

We wondered what drives interior design teams that deliver at scale? Our country has always been home to a plethora of extraordinary design talent; Yet in the process of winning and executing large projects, design itself often takes a back seat.

Execution gets fragmented. Communication becomes chaotic. Vision gets diluted.

Strokes Designs is born to change that! Not for more control, but with much clarity, collaboration, and a deeper respect for design as a craft. We believe the true power of a team is rooted in elevating how firms deliver every day.

We Believe Design Deserves A Better Approach.

While most design processes often feel chaotic, ours is structured and collaborative carefully calibrated and democratic. We ideate, brainstorm, and work together to deliver holistic, thoughtful, and satisfying design solutions.

Why us

We integrate into your firm's culture and systems. We are here to raise the bar, not to reinvent the wheel.

01. Precision

Finalized Design & Approval

Our aesthetical direction, material palette, and design deck are finalized and formally approved prior to execution. This stage ensures that the approved design intent is translated accurately into the built outcome, minimizing delays, rework, or miscommunication during implementation.

02. Systems Ready

Zero Onboarding Friction

We arrive with our systems in place. From initial concept to design deck & material selections. Our combined skillset covers every aspect of design development.

03. Clarity & Intention

End-to-End Execution

At Strokes Designs, everything we do is rooted in clarity and intention. From concept sketching to Revit documentation, FF&E, and construction administration. One team, end to end precision.

04. Disciplined Execution

Detailing & Coordination

We combine creative vision with disciplined execution. Detailing and coordination are built within our process.
