Deutsche Bank HQ

Client

Deutsche Bank

Location

Mumbai, India

Size

180,000 sq.ft

Role

Full Scope

The Deutsche Bank headquarters redesign was an exercise in balancing corporate identity with employee well-being. Spanning multiple floors in the heart of Mumbai, the project required a sophisticated approach to zoning, acoustics, and materiality.

Our team led the entire lifecycle from workplace strategy and stakeholder engagement to detailed documentation and site supervision. The result is a workspace that feels both authoritative and approachable, with flexible collaboration zones interspersed among high-focus work areas.

Key architectural interventions included opening up floor plates to maximize daylight penetration and introducing a central atrium stair to encourage vertical connectivity between departments.
