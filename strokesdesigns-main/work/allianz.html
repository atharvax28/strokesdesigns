Allianz Campus

Client

Allianz

Location

Trivandrum, Kerala

Size

500,000 sq.ft

Role

Full Scope

The Allianz Campus project in Trivandrum represents a massive scale undertaking, spanning 5 Lakh square feet. This large-scale corporate campus was designed to accommodate a significant workforce while maintaining a cohesive and inspiring environment.

The design includes extensive reception and waiting areas, specialized work caf s, training rooms, and vast open office areas. The integration of functional spaces like cafeterias and collaborative zones ensures that the campus supports both focused work and employee well-being.
