Axis Bank HQ

Client

Axis Bank

Location

Mumbai, India

Size

50,000 sq.ft

Role

Full Scope

The Axis Bank headquarters redesign was focused on creating a modern, efficient, and welcoming corporate environment. The objective was to balance the formal nature of a financial institution with contemporary design elements that promote collaboration and transparency.

Key areas such as the reception, boardrooms, and open office spaces were meticulously planned to ensure optimal flow and functionality. The use of premium materials and strategic lighting enhances the overall ambiance, reflecting the bank's stature and commitment to excellence.
