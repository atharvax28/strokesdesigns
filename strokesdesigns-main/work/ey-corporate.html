EY Corporate Offices

Client

Ernst & Young

Location

Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, Bangalore

Size

90,000 sq.ft (Combined)

Role

Full Scope

The Ernst & Young (EY) project involved the design and execution of corporate offices across multiple major cities in India, including Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, and Bangalore. The combined area of these projects totals approximately 90,000 square feet.

The design philosophy centered on creating open office areas that foster collaboration, communication, and flexibility. Strategic zoning provided a balance between openness and private spaces for focused work. The workspaces are tailored to reflect EY's global standards while addressing local functional needs.
